Learn how each quantifier is used in English with clear Amharic translations!

MANY (ብዙ - የሚቆጠሩ)

Use with countable plural nouns (ለሚቆጠሩ ነገሮች)።

Ex: There are many pencils.
(ብዙ እርሳሶች አሉ)

MUCH (ብዙ - የማይቆጠሩ)

Use with uncountable mass nouns (ለማይቆጠሩ ነገሮች)።

Ex: I have much money.
(ብዙ ገንዘብ አለኝ)

SOME (አንዳንድ / ጥቂት)

Use in positive statements (+), or polite offers/requests.

Ex: There are some pencils.
(አንዳንድ እርሳሶች አሉ)

ANY (ምንም / ማናቸውም)

Use in negative statements (-) and questions (?)

Ex: Do you have any money?
(ምንም ገንዘብ አለህ?)

BOTH (ሁለቱም)

Use for exactly TWO persons or objects (ለሁለት ነገሮች/ሰዎች)።

Ex: Both the boy and the girl are my friends.
(ሁለቱም ወንድ እና ሴት ልጆች ጓደኞቼ ናቸው)

ALL (ሁሉም)

Use for THREE or more persons/objects (ለሦስት እና ከዚያ በላይ)።

Ex: I have three friends and I like all of them.
(ሦስት ጓደኞች አሉኝ፤ ሁሉንም እወዳቸዋለሁ)
